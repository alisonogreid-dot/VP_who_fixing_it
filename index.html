Finding a good News source (Slot C)

📰

Try: NRK, BBC, Reuters, AP, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, Mongabay, Down To Earth. Check the date: is it recent enough for your question? Follow the trail: if an article says "a UN report found…", click through. The article goes in Slot C, and the report might be your Slot B.

03Official, News, or Neither?

Before you post, check what kind of source you have. Your credibility note answers four questions:

Who?Who made it? Is the organisation or author named?
Why?To inform, to report, to sell, to campaign?
When?Recent enough for your question?
What's missing?Whose view isn't in it?

Quick check

Sort each source. You'll get feedback straight away.

Notice the pairing: the company's own page (Slot B) says designed to capture 36,000 tonnes. The news investigation (Slot C) checks it. Two sources that disagree are more useful than two that agree.
